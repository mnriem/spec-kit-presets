## NIEM Exchange Governance

Apply these principles to agreed NIEM exchanges, not unrelated internal models.
Reconcile them with existing authored principles through the constitution
command's normal amendment process; report conflicts instead of overwriting them.

1. **Semantics before syntax**: Exchange requirements define shared business
   meanings and producer/consumer obligations before selecting representations.
2. **Verified reuse before extension**: Prefer semantically appropriate Core and
   domain components from the selected model. Document evidence and rationale
   for every local extension; do not imply official NIEM approval.
3. **Explicit baselines**: Record model releases, namespace identities, rule
   editions, and compatibility obligations. Migrations require an explicit
   decision, not merely a newer available release.
4. **Traceable delivery**: Link information requirements to mappings, contracts,
   implementation tasks, and the agreed evidence.
5. **Evidence-qualified claims**: Distinguish requirements review, model/schema
   conformance, message validity, business correctness, and interoperability.
   Required checks and unavailable evidence must be explicit.
6. **Responsible sharing**: Use synthetic or approved sanitized examples and
   honor exchange-specific access, retention, and disclosure requirements.

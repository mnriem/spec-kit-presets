## NIEM Requirements Quality

For exchanges in scope, derive requirements-quality questions from the following
topics. Use the base checklist's sequential IDs, traceability references, and
unchecked-item format; this is not an executable validation report.

- Are exchange participants, boundaries, and information meanings explicit?
- Is NIEM adoption a documented requirement or decision rather than an assumption?
- Are applicable release and compatibility obligations stated at the appropriate
  requirements/design level?
- Are required/optional information, identity, code meanings, and invalid or
  unsupported inputs unambiguous?
- Are sharing restrictions and handling of sensitive example data specified?
- Are the evidence and acceptance criteria for each conformance or
  interoperability claim defined?
- Do requirements distinguish semantic correctness from schema validity?

Do not write implementation-verification items such as "run the schema validator"
or mark reviewer-owned items complete. If NIEM is out of scope, omit these topics
or state not applicable according to the base checklist's conventions.
